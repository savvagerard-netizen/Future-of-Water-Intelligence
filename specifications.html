Contamination Removal Capabilities

Comprehensive elimination of organic, chemical, and heavy metal contaminants.

Organic Contaminants Eliminated

  • Bacteria & pathogens (all types)
  • Viruses & viral organisms
  • Sewage & fecal matter
  • Algae & harmful algal blooms (HABs)
  • Biofilm & slime layers
  • All organic matter

Chemical Contaminants Treated

  • PFAS (per- & polyfluoroalkyl substances)
  • Pharmaceuticals & medication residues
  • Pesticides & herbicides
  • Volatile organic compounds (VOCs)
  • Microplastics & nano-contaminants
  • Industrial chemical residues

Heavy Metals & Nutrients

  • Heavy metals (arsenic, lead, mercury)
  • PFAS molecules & related compounds
  • Nitrogen reduction (N removal)
  • Phosphorus reduction (P removal)
  • Industrial waste contaminants
  • Water quality optimization

Treatment Characteristics

  • Only residual: dissolved oxygen
  • No chemical byproducts
  • No secondary waste streams
  • No disinfection byproducts
  • Continued treatment (≈20 days post-application)
  • Prevents bacterial resistance buildup
