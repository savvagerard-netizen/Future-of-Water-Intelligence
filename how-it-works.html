Understanding NBOT Technology

How It Works

NBOT harnesses nano-bubble physics to generate hydroxyl radicals—the most powerful oxidizers in water treatment—precisely at the point of contamination.

The NBOT Four-Stage Process

A paradigm shift in advanced oxidation powered by patented nano-bubble technology.

1

Ozone Infusion

NBOT infuses ozone into water as ultra-small, stable nanobubbles—each carrying an O₃ payload that remains suspended far longer than conventional macro-bubbles.

2

Nano-Bubble Delivery

Nanobubbles remain suspended throughout the full water volume, dramatically extending ozone contact time compared to traditional bubble systems.

3

Targeted Contact

Negatively charged nanobubbles are electrostatically attracted to contaminants—PFAS molecules, microplastics, bacteria, organic compounds—maximizing contact.

4

Instant Oxidation

As ozone nanobubbles decompose, they trigger O₃ + H₂O → •OH + O₂, releasing powerful hydroxyl radicals that react immediately with contaminants.

Why This Matters: The Differentiator

Hydroxyl Radical Power

Hydroxyl radicals are one of the strongest oxidants known to science—over 100,000 times stronger than chlorine, and 2,000× stronger than ozone alone.

Second only to fluorine in oxidative power, hydroxyl radicals exist for fractions of a second, reacting immediately with contaminants.

Precise Generation

NBOT generates hydroxyl radicals precisely at the point of contamination—not in bulk solution. This ensures no oxidation potential is wasted.

How Hydroxyl Radicals Destroy Contaminants

Destroys Cell Nucleus & DNA

Hydroxyl radicals penetrate cell membranes, destroying the nucleus and DNA of bacteria, viruses, and microorganisms—preventing any possibility of resistance buildup.

Breaks Molecular Bonds

Destroys the functional groups within organic molecules—carboxylic acids, amines, sulfides, phosphates—completely dismantling contaminant structures.

Leaves Only Oxygen

The only residual byproduct is dissolved oxygen—completely safe, no chemical footprint, no secondary treatment required.

Residual Treatment Effect

Treatment benefits continue for approximately 20 days post-application (at 45°C), depending on specific deployment conditions and water chemistry.
