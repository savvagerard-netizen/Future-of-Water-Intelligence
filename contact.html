    Frequently Asked Questions

    How long does an assessment take?

    Comprehensive assessments typically take 2-4 weeks, including site analysis, water quality testing, and customized recommendations.

    How quickly can NBOT be deployed?

    Modular design enables rapid deployment. Installation typically completes in 2-8 weeks depending on site preparation and integration complexity.

    What's the cost compared to traditional treatment?

    NBOT typically reduces operating costs by 30-50% through lower chemical consumption and reduced maintenance. Request an assessment for detailed ROI analysis.

    Is NBOT compatible with existing systems?

    Yes. NBOT drops into most existing tank systems with minimal civil works. Our engineering team designs the optimal integration approach.

    How is NBOT monitored after deployment?

    24/7 cloud-based monitoring with AI-driven predictive maintenance. Real-time dashboards provide performance visibility and automated alerts.
